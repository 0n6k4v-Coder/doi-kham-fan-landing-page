Doi Kham tomato juice is made to bring the natural goodness of carefully selected tomatoes into a refreshing and enjoyable everyday drink.

The tomatoes are grown through farming partnerships in Thailand, connecting the product with local agricultural communities and fresh harvests.

The product is presented as a natural tomato-based drink, designed to preserve the fresh character and vibrant taste of the tomatoes.

Chill before serving and enjoy it as a refreshing drink on its own or as part of your daily routine.

Follow the storage instructions provided on the product packaging and refrigerate after opening when required.
